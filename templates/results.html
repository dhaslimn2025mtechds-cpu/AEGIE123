Research Interpretation

AEGIE currently demonstrates an end-to-end research prototype for adaptive agentic voice interview evaluation. The implemented pipeline captures candidate speech, transcribes it using Parakeet, extracts measurable speech features, computes MiniLM-based semantic evidence, and coordinates evaluation, adaptation, and feedback through specialized agents.

The current DEVELOPMENT configuration uses rule-based adaptation and descriptive semantic evidence. Human annotation, calibration, agreement analysis, and held-out validation are required before semantic evidence can be converted into scientifically supported interview scores.
